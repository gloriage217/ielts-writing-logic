# 方法浓缩版


### 一、题型识别与立场决策（30 秒流程）

四大题型：

| 题型 | 题干信号 | 你要回答什么 |
|---|---|---|
| 同意与否 | To what extent do you agree or disagree? | 明确立场 + 论证 |
| 双边讨论 | Discuss both views and give your opinion | 双方都写 + 自己立场 |
| 正负发展 | Is this a positive or negative development? | 利弊权衡 + 结论 |
| 分析解决 | What are the problems/causes? What measures? | 原因 + 建议 |

立场决策树：

```
A 和 B 能不能同时成立？
├── 能 → 共存式：combine with / the joint effort / work together
│      （或按时间拆分：short term 靠 A，long term 靠 B）
└── 不能 → 偏向式：虽然 A，但是 B
       ① 指出对方的极端表达不准确
       ② 题干有最高级（the best / the only way）→ 先合理化：承认有价值，再降维反驳
       ③ 高低逻辑：A 也好，但 A 有它解决不了、B 能解决的问题
```

铁律：**观点 ≠ 例子**。能当主题句的是观点，只能放在 for example 后面的是例子。

### 二、开头段：改写四法 + 对比式立场

开头只做两件事：改写题目 + 亮立场。

改写四法：①换同义词；②换词性；③概括↔具体；④换固定搭配。

同义链（背）：

- 承担：undertake / shoulder / assume / carry on
- 政府：government / authority / administration
- 责任：duty / responsibility / obligation / commitment
- should do → be supposed / advised / expected to do

对比式立场句（背前三个）：

1. Many people argue that [A], but some think otherwise, saying [B].
2. Despite the widespread belief that [A], many are skeptical about its validity. They argue that [B].
3. While some people advocate for [A], others question its effectiveness. They contend that [B].
4. Although proponents of [A] claim that it brings numerous benefits, critics argue that [B] is more advantageous.
5. Even though [A] has gained popularity, there are still those who doubt its feasibility.
6. 亮立场：However, I firmly believe (that) …

### 三、SEESC 五步论证法（主体段引擎）

| 步骤 | 功能 | 常用引出词 |
|---|---|---|
| S 论点 | 一段只立一个论点 | There is no doubt that… / The primary benefit is… |
| E 剖析 | 因果链 | This is because… / In other words… |
| E 例证 | 2–3 句封顶，结尾回扣论点 | For instance… / A prime example of this is… |
| S 置换 | 换条件再推一遍 | if / once / provided that / supposing / but for / without / unless |
| C 小结 | 回扣论点 | Therefore… / As a consequence… |

置换推理延展维度：程度、范围、领域、对象、时间；外加反向假设（如果不这样，会怎样）。

语法升级（每篇至少用一次）：think / find / make + it + adj + to do / that…

### 四、双边段与让步驳论

正方段开头：Those who [stand up for / side with / are in favor of / are strong advocates of] … [argue / insist / maintain / assert] that …

反方段开头：The objections of … come mainly from those who [claim / contend / are convinced] that …

论据推进：To begin with / First and foremost / For one thing → Moreover / Besides / What is more / For another

反方收尾可用一次反问：What is the point of …?

让步驳论三步：①立靶子 It is undoubtful / indubitable that …（只让一句）→ ②翻盘 However, this view is flawed. → ③升维：换更高层次证明对方不够，然后落到具体。

### 五、分析解决型与正负发展型骨架

原因段五步：

1. There are some practical reasons why …
2. The biggest concern is that …（主因）
3. A further example comes mainly from …, where …（具象化）
4. Another situation involves … / Hardly can one more factor remain untouched.
5. That is to say, …（把原因讲透）

建议段五步：

1. In terms of the [feasible / workable] solution to …
2. 主语 be [well advised / highly recommended] to do …
3. A further example comes mainly from …（他国经验）
4. It is high time for … to do / that …（虚拟，从句用过去式）
5. By doing so, …（描绘效果）

正负发展型四段：开头让步先行 → 优势段（优势 + 旧方案劣势对比着写）→ 缺点段 → 权衡结论：Although … may not be flawless, its advantages in terms of … far outweigh its limitations.

### 六、例证与思路库（浓缩）

八大人物：Jobs（创新）/ Confucius（教育）/ Roosevelt（经济干预）/ Martin Luther King Jr.（领导力）/ Greta Thunberg（环保）/ Nightingale（医学）/ Gutenberg（传播）/ Malala（女性教育）。

嵌入模板：A prime example of this is [人物], who [成就]. His/Her contribution highlights [观点]. This demonstrates that [论点].

政府干预三维：手段（宣传 / 加税 / 补贴 / 设施 / 立法）；动机（人性自私 / 费用门槛 / 经济账 / 自控力不足）；领域（环保 / 运动饮食 / 公共场所行为）。

### 七、语言升级包

- 倒装：Hardly can one more factor remain untouched.
- Not only 前置：Not only are the costs high, but they also require regular maintenance.
- 虚拟：It is high time that the government took action.
- 权衡：far outweigh its limitations
- 高级模板句（Within the vibrant tapestry of contemporary discourse…）仅限能改写贴题时使用，全文至少一半句子必须是你自己的论证。
