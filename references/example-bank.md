# 事例与思路库

## 八人物事例库（人物｜主题｜成就句｜影响句）

1. Steve Jobs｜创新｜co-founder of Apple, revolutionized the technology industry by introducing user-friendly devices such as the iPhone｜reshaped the way people communicate and access information
2. Confucius｜教育｜ancient Chinese philosopher and educator, emphasized moral education, lifelong learning and respect for elders｜his teachings continue to influence educational systems worldwide
3. Franklin D. Roosevelt｜经济｜during the Great Depression, introduced the New Deal, a series of economic reforms aimed at creating jobs and stabilizing the economy｜large-scale public works revitalized the U.S. economy
4. Martin Luther King Jr.｜领导力｜played a pivotal role in the civil rights movement by advocating racial equality through peaceful protests｜"I Have a Dream" remains a symbol of hope and justice
5. Greta Thunberg｜环保｜Swedish environmental activist, raised global awareness of climate change through speeches and climate strikes｜influenced millions of young people and world leaders to act
6. Florence Nightingale｜医学｜founder of modern nursing, transformed hospital care by emphasizing hygiene and patient well-being｜significantly reduced mortality rates in hospitals
7. Johannes Gutenberg｜传播｜invented the printing press in the 15th century, making books more accessible｜increased literacy rates and facilitated the dissemination of knowledge
8. Malala Yousafzai｜女性教育｜Pakistani activist for girls' education, survived an assassination attempt and continued to advocate for the right to education｜youngest Nobel Prize laureate

备用：Elon Musk｜个人主义/创新｜founder of SpaceX, reduced the cost of space travel and made reusable rockets a reality｜individual pursuit created widespread benefits far beyond personal goals

## 嵌入模板

- A prime example of this is [人物], who [成就]. His/Her contribution highlights [观点]. This demonstrates that [论点].
- One historical figure that supports this argument is [人物], whose work in [领域] transformed [影响]. This proves that [观点].

铁律：例子是子弹，回扣句是扳机——例子末句必须出现论点关键词，否则例子只是故事。

## 博物馆话题思路（原因侧/对策侧）

原因：展品长期不更新 / 内容本地人早已熟悉 / 营销对准旅游业 / 票价贵且开放时间与上班族冲突 / 现代娱乐竞争 / 缺乏互动体验

对策：定期临展与国际合作 / 本地居民年卡与免费开放日 / 社区讲座工作坊进校园 / VR、AR 科技互动 / 本地文化节与亲子活动 / 周末节假日延长开放

用法：思路是中文提纲，进作文必须转写成完整英文论点句，带对比或因果（一条思路 = 一个 S+E）。
