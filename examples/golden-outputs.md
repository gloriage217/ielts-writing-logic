# Golden Output Walkthroughs

These are compact demonstrations, not four replacement model essays. Each starts with a plausible learner input and shows what the skill makes visible: the route for the question type, the paragraph's logic, and one clear learning outcome.

## 1. Agree / disagree

### Input

> **Question:** People are annoyed or entertained by advertising, but advertisements do not affect their shopping decisions. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
>
> **Learner's idea:** I disagree because advertisements help people know products and can make them buy things they do not need.

### What the skill shows

**Route:** Agree / disagree. Take a clear degree of disagreement; do not turn it into a discussion of two equal sides.

| Check | Target | Learner's next move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TR | Answer whether advertising affects decisions, not merely whether it exists. | Explain *how* repeated exposure turns awareness into a purchase. |
| CC | Use two distinct reasons. | Separate useful information from emotional manipulation. |

```text
Logic map: S → E → E → S2 → C

[S Claim] Advertising often influences shopping decisions, even when consumers believe that they are choosing independently.
[E Explanation] Repeated exposure makes a brand more familiar and therefore easier to recall at the point of purchase.
[E Example] For example, a shopper who repeatedly sees an advertisement for a delivery app may select it when ordering food simply because its name comes to mind first.
[S2 Substitution] Without this constant exposure, the same consumer would be more likely to compare unfamiliar alternatives on price or quality.
[C Link-back] Advertising therefore shapes decisions indirectly as well as informing consumers.
```

**中文逻辑：** 先反驳题干的核心断言；再讲“反复曝光 → 品牌熟悉 → 购买时优先想起”的因果；例子只负责把因果落地；`Without` 句把条件拿掉再推一次，证明广告确实发挥了作用。

**Learner takes away:** 同意与否题要先回应“同不同意”，主体段不是罗列广告现象，而是证明你的同意程度。

## 2. Discuss both views

### Input

> **Question:** Some people say that the best way to improve public health is by increasing the number of sports facilities. Others say that other measures are required. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
>
> **Learner's idea:** Sports facilities are good, but the government should also do something else.

### What the skill shows

**Route:** Discuss both views. This is where the A/B decision applies. “Sports facilities” and “other measures” can coexist, but the word *best* needs testing: facilities help active people, while diet, smoking and access to care require other tools.

```text
Logic map: [Concession] → [S Claim] → E → E → S2 → C

[Concession] Building affordable sports facilities can remove a practical barrier to exercise.
[S Claim] However, treating this as the best solution is too narrow because poor health has several causes beyond inactivity.
[E Explanation] Unhealthy diets and tobacco use can damage health even among people who have convenient access to gyms or parks.
[E Example] For instance, a resident may exercise regularly but still face a high risk of illness if cheap processed food and cigarettes remain widely available.
[S2 Substitution] If governments combined facilities with nutrition education and tobacco controls, they would address both physical inactivity and preventable lifestyle risks.
[C Link-back] Sports facilities should therefore form part of a broader public-health strategy rather than replace it.
```

**中文逻辑：** 先承认体育设施有价值，避免把正方写成“完全错误”；再抓住 `the best`，把它降为“有用但不够”；最后用“组合政策”说明为什么更全面的方案更优。

**Learner takes away:** 双边题不是机械地“一段支持 A、一段支持 B”，而是先判断 A/B 的关系，再让自己的 opinion 控制两段的分量。

## 3. Positive / negative development

### Input

> **Question:** Fossil fuels are the main source of energy today. In some countries, alternative energy sources are replacing them. Is this a positive or negative development?
>
> **Learner's idea:** This is positive because renewable energy is cleaner, but it is expensive.

### What the skill shows

**Route:** Positive / negative development. Weigh the two sides; do not use the A/B decision tree for a discussion prompt that does not exist.

```text
Logic map: [S Benefit] → E → E → [Concession] → [Weighing]

[S Benefit] Replacing fossil fuels with renewable sources is broadly a positive development because it can reduce reliance on high-emission energy.
[E Explanation] Solar and wind power generate electricity without the routine combustion that releases large quantities of greenhouse gases and harmful air pollutants.
[E Example] As clean capacity expands, households and businesses can meet more of their energy demand without burning coal or oil.
[Concession] Admittedly, installing renewable infrastructure can require substantial initial investment and may create local environmental trade-offs.
[Weighing] Nevertheless, these manageable costs are outweighed by the long-term benefits of cleaner air, lower emissions and more sustainable energy supplies.
```

**中文逻辑：** 正负题的立场不是“只夸优点”。先把优势讲完整，再给缺点一个真实位置，最后比较两边的分量；`outweighed by` 就是权衡动作，不是装饰词。

**Learner takes away:** 正负题的高分感来自“承认缺点后仍能证明主结论”，不是从头到尾单方面吹捧。

## 4. Causes / problems + solutions

### Input

> **Question:** In many cities, traffic congestion has become increasingly severe. What are the causes of this problem? What measures can be taken to solve it?
>
> **Learner's idea:** Traffic is caused by too many cars. The government should improve public transport.

### What the skill shows

**Route:** Causes / problems + solutions. Diagnose before prescribing, then make the correspondence visible.

```text
Cause map: private-car dependence → overloaded roads
Solution map: reliable public transport + demand controls → fewer car journeys

[Cause] A central cause of urban congestion is that rising incomes enable more households to own private cars.
[Explanation] When public transport is slow or unreliable, commuters have little incentive to leave those cars at home, so roads become overloaded at peak times.

[Corresponding solution] Cities should invest in frequent, reliable public transport while using parking charges or congestion fees to discourage unnecessary car journeys.
[Effect] If these measures make buses and rail more convenient than driving, a substantial share of commuters can switch modes and road pressure will fall.
```

**中文逻辑：** 不能只写“车多”和“修公交”两个孤立句。先解释“为什么车多”——公交不可靠使人不得不开车；再让建议同时解决“替代选择不足”和“开车成本过低”两个原因。原因讲什么，建议就必须治什么。

**Learner takes away:** 分析解决题最重要的不是列很多建议，而是让阅卷者清楚看见“这个药正好治前面诊断出的这个病”。

## Revision principle: improve the learner's writing, not replace it

The skill keeps the learner's defensible stance, paragraph order and usable ideas. It labels the role of each sentence, explains the reasoning in Chinese, and then supplies two clearly different outputs:

1. **Integrated revision:** repairs task response, logic, language and paragraph structure where needed.
2. **Polish version:** retains the learner's original stance and core ideas, improving grammar, collocation, cohesion and sentence control only.

If an original argument misunderstands the question, the skill says so explicitly. It does not quietly solve the problem by submitting an unrelated, polished model essay.
